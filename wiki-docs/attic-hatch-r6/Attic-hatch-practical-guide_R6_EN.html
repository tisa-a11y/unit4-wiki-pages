Fit the frame, ledges and lid

Non-rated wood option only. Pre-assemble square on the bench and keep all latch hardware outside the 24 × 36 passage.

4Build a square lining frame: Set the two 25-1/2 in. ends between the two 39 in. sides (27 × 39 in. outside). Pre-drill and drive two #8 × 2 in. screws per corner; verify equal diagonals before mounting.
5Shim and anchor frame flush to ceiling: Align bottom edge flush with finished landing ceiling. Place solid wood shims at screw points and drive four #9 × 3 in. screws per long side and three per short end into verified framing without bowing inward.
6Install the four 1×2 lid ledges: Stand 1×2 strips on edge (3/4 in. inward projection, 1-1/2 in. vertical recess). Glue, pre-drill, and fasten with four #8 × 1-1/4 in. screws per long strip and three per short strip. Re-verify full 24 × 36 in. clear between ledges.
7Fit uninsulated 3/4" lid, pulls, gasket & 4 latches: Dry-fit the 25-1/4 × 37-1/4 in. plywood lid (1/8 in. even gap). Mount 2 recessed folding ring pulls and 4 compression latches outside the clear opening. Apply continuous 1/2 × 1/8 in. closed-cell foam gasket on clean ledge tops; test smooth lift, tilt, and safe parking away from the stairwell.
