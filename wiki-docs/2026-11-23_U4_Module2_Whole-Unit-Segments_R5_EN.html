How the Two Independent Mappings Work

  • Mapping 1 — Physical Box / Switch / Fixture Name (Dark Tag): Fixed physical location on the wall or ceiling (East 1, Under-Sink 2-Gang, Entry 3-Switch, Dining 1–4, Vanity, Toilet, Washer/Dryer, Bath J-Box). Never changes if circuits are split later; one multi-gang box can hold multiple circuits.
  • Mapping 2 — Assigned Circuit & Cable (#1–#20 Pill): Eaton BRP20L125G subpanel slot + Romex color/gauge: 12/2 Yellow = 20A (#1–#5, #10 new drops, #13, #14) · 14/2 White = 15A (#6–#9, #15, #16) · #11, #12 & #17–#20 = 6 Open Spares (reused lights/switches & reused outlets stay on Existing Panel = 0 subpanel spaces!).

Active Holds & Code Guardrails (Read First)

  • Panel & Feeder Hold (Mon Nov 23): Reconcile NEXT 80A/#4 vs. 100A/#3 feeder & hold side-entry notches on Panel (X=147", Y=213.75"/214", faces Dining; 30"W×36"D×78"H clear per SFEC 110.26(A)(3); ZERO back-entries or screws 53"–77" AFF on Kitchen East wall).
  • Reused Wire Gauge Gate (#10 Shared): Verify gauge of EVERY reused wire leg (NEC 240.4(D)(3)): if ANY reused segment is 14 AWG, install a 15A AFCI (BRN115AF) breaker (never 20A!).
  • SFEC 362.10 vs. Non-Kitchen Hold: SFEC 362.10 restricts ENT in wood walls whereas SFEC 334.10(2) permits concealed NM-B; preserve task 6hvRcQ7Gmh3fG5m7 NO-WIRE-PULL hold until owner/inspector release.
  • South Window CL & Pocket-Door Holds: Verify Kitchen South window CL (87" vs. 90" W of SE) before fastening South 2 / Under-Sink 2-Gang; ZERO boxes inside ~33" pocket-door cavities (Dining D6 / Living L6).
