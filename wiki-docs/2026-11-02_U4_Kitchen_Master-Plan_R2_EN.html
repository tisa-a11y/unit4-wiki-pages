4 · The whole job, start to finish

Durations are typical estimates, not appointments. No step is "done" until there is a dated photo or measurement.

Step 1 · Lock and mark out (this week)

  • You make decisions D1–D3.
  • Crew tapes every cabinet and appliance footprint on the subfloor, starting at the SE corner. South wall uses the new chain: 0, 36, 51, 75, 105, about 112; sink center 90 (do not use K002's south run). East and west from K002. John or you check the marks against the plan.
  • John records the floor datum: the difference between the subfloor and the future finished floor (depends on D4), and the dining/kitchen floor step.
  • John does the D04 checks: C1 W, C2 jamb positions, C3 header height and floor step, C4 plaster thickness and stud depth.

Step 2 · Framing (John · about 2–4 days once released)

  • Close the old right doorway per R1. The 14½ in panel bay stays open until the can and cover are on site (C5).
  • Rebuild the pie-safe niche framing to the D6 target.
  • West range wall: hood blocking, a clear stud bay straight up for the 6 in duct, and the chimney box framing if you choose it in D9.
  • Add framing or blocking for the 3 ceiling boxes (main light and 3 task lights over the sink run at 18, 54 and 90 in from the SE corner, 15 in off the south wall).

Step 3 · Trace what's hidden (John or trades · can run in parallel with step 2)

  • Old west-wall metal pipes: find where they go, including whether they connect downstairs, only so they can be capped or removed. The new hood gets its own duct (section 2). Mark the roof cap spot directly above the hood, checking the attic rafters first.
  • Gas: check the range stub position against the Viking VGCC5304BSS installation sheet (on file as S10).
  • Plumbing: record the actual sink drain, vent and supply positions. The new sink center is 90 in (24 in west of the old 66), directly under the window, so the vent cannot rise straight up there: the plumber plans the offset. The dishwasher (left of the sink) drains into the sink base with a high loop; plan the disposal.
  • Old wiring: trace the knob-and-tube that serves the kitchen. Remove it or isolate it before the cabinets go in.

Step 4 · Blocking and backing (John · about 1 day)

  • Hood: blocking per the Zephyr manual (S11) in the west alcove.
  • Uppers insurance: continuous 2× blocking bands on the east and south walls, roughly 52–58 in and 90–98 in above the subfloor, running one stud bay past the possible upper footprint. This is a proposal for John to confirm. It means D7 never holds up drywall.
  • Dishwasher side and top anchoring, the corner-base cleat, and the filler and counter end support at the west end of the south run (about 112 in from the SE corner).

Step 5 · Rough-ins (electrician, plumber, gas · about 3–5 days once released)

  • Electrical (needs the panel, D2): install the subpanel and the 100 A copper feeder in conduit. Run the kitchen circuits:
    • Countertops, 3 circuits: east E1/E2, south S1 (36 in, 40 AFF) and S2 (66½ in, 44 AFF), west W1/W2.
    • Fridge, on an AFCI/GFCI breaker.
    • Range.
    • Dishwasher and disposal: one 2-gang box in the sink base at 79 in, 14 in AFF, two receptacles on two circuits, each with its own neutral.
    • Hood, hardwired.
    • Lighting, one circuit, with Lutron controls at the entry and a remote at the sink.
    Box positions come from the appliance manuals. Tiled zones get 4×4 boxes with 1 in rings. The electrician signs off locations before boxes go in.
  • Open item for the electrician: the Bosch manual says 15 A and the older plan says 20 A. Confirm from the label number (E-Nr).
  • Plumbing: sink supply and drain at the new center 90 (vent offset around the window), dishwasher supply, and the disposal. No fridge water line is planned.
  • Hood duct: install the new 6 in rigid duct and roof cap now, while everything is open (roofer flashes the cap).
  • Gas: range stub inside the 0½–5 in zone, with a reachable shut-off. Licensed gas fitter, leak test.

Step 6 · Inspections and pre-drywall walk (the gate)

  • Rough inspections as required (framing, electrical, plumbing and gas). Who pulls the permits isn't recorded yet. See the gaps below.
  • Walk every wall with the sheets and photograph each one with a tape in frame. Nothing closes until every rough-in item is done, capped and labeled, or waived by you.

Step 7 · Close-in (about 3–5 days plus drying)

  • Insulate the exterior walls and ceiling. Firestop all penetrations.
  • Drywall, tape and prime. Use cement board or a tile backer in the alcove (and the backsplash, if D5 includes it).
  • Finish the pie-safe lining.

Step 8 · Floor (about 2–3 days plus cure)

  • Per D4: floor the open area plus under the fridge, dishwasher and range. Use shims or riser strips under the cabinets so the counter stays at 36 in and the dishwasher still fits. John checks this against the Bosch install manual (S07) before setting.
  • Protect the floor.

Step 9 · Cabinets (about 2–3 days)

  • If the cabinets are painted rather than prefinished, paint them before installing.
  • Set the corner base first, then build outward on both walls. Level, shim, fasten to blocking and fit fillers.
  • Check that the doors, pull-outs, the dishwasher door and fridge doors all open freely.

Step 10 · Countertop (template → about 1–2 weeks to fabricate → install)

  • Template once the cabinets are fixed. Fabricate with the sink and range cutouts, then install the countertop and mount the sink.

Step 11 · Tile and finishes (about 2–4 days)

  • Tile the alcove and backsplash (per D5). John lays it out first.
  • Paint the walls and finish the trim and pie-safe casing.

Step 12 · Trim-out and appliances (about 2–3 days)

  • Install devices, the 3 ceiling fixtures and under-cabinet lights if uppers go in.
  • Install the faucet, disposal and dishwasher (anchored, high loop).
  • Connect the range gas (leak test) and install the hood.
  • Set the fridge with its 2 in rear gap.

Step 13 · Final

  • Final inspections, test every circuit and GFCI, check for leaks, punch list, clean.
