ElementCurrent requirement · Nov. 24, 2026
Permanent hatch location

24" × 36" finished clear outside Front Door 4 on the unconditioned common stair landing.

Uninsulated lid & gasket

Unconditioned Door 4 landing: 2025 CA Energy Code §160.1(a).

Use a snug 3/4" plywood lid; 3/8" backer rod and caulk at the fixed frame; continuous 1/2" × 1/8" closed-cell foam gasket on 1×2 ledges; and 4 latches outside the clear opening.

No fiberglass lid insulation, wire loops, eye-bolts, or perimeter barrier.

Horizontal sidewall exhaust only: ≥ 4 ft from the lot line under 2025 SFMC §802.8. No flat-roof penetrations.
