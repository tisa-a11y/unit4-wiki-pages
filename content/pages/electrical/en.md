# Electrical

## Concept
New Eaton panel in the dining-room wall. It feeds the kitchen, bathroom, attic and room outlets.

## Panel, feeder and circuits
- **Panel:** the Eaton box is on site. NEXT HVAC installs it.
- **Panel wall:** do not close it. Cable enters from the side.
- **Kitchen:** nine circuits. The hood has its own.
- **Bathroom:** two GFCI outlet boxes.
- **Attic:** light with a switch, and an outlet that stays live.
- **Bedroom, living, dining, hallway:** outlet boxes and empty conduit only. No wire yet.
- **Bedroom closet:** switch cable up the left wall to the attic.
