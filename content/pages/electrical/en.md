# Electrical

## Concept
New Eaton panel in the dining-room wall. It feeds the kitchen, bathroom, attic and room outlets.

## Panel, feeder and circuits
- **Panel:** the Eaton box is on site. NEXT HVAC installs it.
- **Panel wall:** do not close it. Cable enters from the side.
- **Interactive Kitchen Electrical &amp; Panel D04 Plan:** Open the [Interactive Kitchen Electrical &amp; Panel D04 Plan](./plans/kitchen-electrical/index.html) ([Hood Test-Hang Wiring &amp; Photos Sheet](#doc:2026-11-20_U4_kitchen_zephyr-savona-mock-hookup_04-hood-electrical_R2_EN)) for all four kitchen walls, the 20-space breaker map, wire runs, and box positions.
- **Kitchen:** nine circuits. The hood has its own dedicated 15-amp AFCI circuit wired straight into the hood top via a flush 1-gang 2" × 3" box ~83"–86" from finished floor (never a 4" × 4" box at 100"). Kitchen West wall uses purchased **1/2" drywall** meeting 1/2" HardieBacker with no stud shims.
- **Bathroom:** two GFCI outlet boxes.
- **Attic:** light with a switch, and an outlet that stays live.
- **Bedroom, living, dining, hallway:** outlet boxes and empty conduit only. No wire yet.
- **Bedroom closet:** switch cable up the left wall to the attic.
