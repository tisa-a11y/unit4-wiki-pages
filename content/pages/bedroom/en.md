# Unit 4 Bedroom

## Concept
Edwardian bedroom with picture-frame wall mouldings aligned to the existing `9-1/2"` baseboard and `99"` high trim line, keeping the East bay window and West closet niche clean.

## Wall Orientation & Trim Method
- **Canonical Walls:** **Wall A** = North (headboard) · **Wall B** = East (bay window, **no frames**) · **Wall C** = South (shared with living room, formerly Wall B) · **Wall D** = West (entry & plain closet niche with rod).
- **Tape-Fit Method (R2 Amendment):** Hold all moulding cuts until profile release. Tape-fit frames from each wall's centerline, aligned to existing baseboard and high trim.
- **Temporary Attic Access & Flooring:** Keep closet ceiling open until low-voltage switch cable **AND** permanent `24"×36"` hallway hatch exist. Repair missing hardwood floor patch and set outlet boxes + empty ENT smurf tube.
