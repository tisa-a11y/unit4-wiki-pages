# Bedroom

## Concept
An Edwardian bedroom with picture-frame mouldings on the walls, lined up with the existing 9-1/2" baseboard and the trim line at 99". The bay window wall and the closet niche stay plain.

## Walls and trim
- **Wall names:** Wall A is North (headboard). Wall B is East (bay window, no frames). Wall C is South (shared with the living room; older drawings call it Wall B). Wall D is West (entry door and the plain closet niche with a rod).
- **Mouldings:** do not cut any moulding yet. Once the moulding choice is released, lay out the frames with tape from the center of each wall, lined up with the baseboard and the high trim. Use the R2 trim packet together with its tape-fit note.
- **Closet ceiling:** keep it open. It is the temporary way into the attic until the low-voltage switch cable is in and the permanent hallway hatch exists.
- **Also:** patch the missing piece of hardwood floor, and set outlet boxes with empty flexible conduit (smurf tube).
