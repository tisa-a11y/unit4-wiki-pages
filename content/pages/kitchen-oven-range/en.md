# Kitchen → Oven / Range Run — Viking 30" Range & Zephyr Savona ZSA-E30AS Hood

## Concept
West range wall featuring the 30-inch **Viking `VGCC5304BSS`** gas range and 30-inch stainless **Zephyr Savona `ZSA-E30AS`** chimney hood with ceiling-height statement tile (**no upper cabinets at the hood**).

## Verified Engineering & Mounting Specs (Viking + Zephyr OEM)
- **Governing Mounting Height (`30"–36"` above range = `66"–72"` AFF):** **Viking `VGCC5304BSS` installation spec (`30"–36"` above cooking surface = `66"–72"` AFF at `36"` counter)** governs over Zephyr's `28"` minimum (`64"` AFF fails Viking spec by `2"`). Canopy mounting bracket screws (Line B) sit `9-9/16"` above hood bottom (Line A) at `9-27/32"` horizontal spread with solid wood blocking (`≥ 1-1/2"` wood embedment across dual backing zones `68"–86"` AFF and `104"–121-1/2"` AFF + floor buildup `t`).
- **Viking Rear Clearance & Backguard Rule:** With the **`1"` Island Trim**, Viking requires **`6"` rear clearance to combustibles** behind the range (tile over drywall/wood studs is not a CSA non-combustible assembly without AHJ approval); using the **`8"` High Backguard** allows **`0"` rear clearance** to above-counter wall surfaces (with a `3/4"` rear standoff depth). Side trim sits `3/8"` above countertop.
- **West Wall Width (`K-S4` vs. Alcove Basis):** Reconcile `K-S4` flat `66-1/4"` run (`18"` base + `30"` range + `18"` base with `1/8"`/side scribe) against `42"` alcove / `4"` pilasters (`50"` total, leaving `8-1/8"`/side unless pilasters counter-mount with `≥ 6"` side clearance to burners).
- **Dedicated Electrical (`15A` AFCI `Circuit HD`):** `120V / 60Hz`, max `415W / 3.4A` (`> 300 VA` CEC `210.23(A)(2)` — requires dedicated **`15A` AFCI `Circuit HD` with `CEC 422.31(B)` breaker lock-off**; cannot share lighting circuit `LT`, no solid-state speed control). Keep junction box (`K-S7` `100"` AFF) inside the `8-5/8"` wide upper cover clear of the `6"` round duct + `1"` mud rings. Range uses `1/2"` gas shutoff + `120V` grounded receptacle.
- **Vertical `6"` Rigid Duct & `121-1/2"` Ceiling Gap:** Vertical exhaust only with **`6"` round rigid metal duct** (zero flex duct), `685 CFM` (`> 400 CFM` makeup-air coordination before final commissioning). Stock telescopic duct cover fits max **`116"–117"` ceiling**, leaving a **`4-1/2"` to `13-1/2"` ceiling gap** at the `121-1/2"` ceiling (Option A top soffit/chase vs. Option B `Z1C-00SA` extension).
