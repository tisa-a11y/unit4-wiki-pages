# Kitchen

## Concept
L-shaped kitchen. Dark green lower cabinets, brass hardware, light counters. Tile to the ceiling behind the range. No upper cabinets beside the hood.

## Room and walls
- **Room:** 147" × 126". Ceiling 121-1/2".
- **West wall:** range and hood. See [Oven / Range Run](#kitchen-oven-range).
- **South wall:** sink under the window, pie-safe shelves. See [Sink & North Wall Runs](#kitchen-sink-north).
- **East wall:** 88" run ending at the refrigerator.
- **North wall:** hall opening, three light switches, electrical panel wall.
- **Floor:** measure the finished floor thickness (about 3/4") before marking cabinet heights.
