# Unit 4 Kitchen — Overview & Layout

## Concept
A custom L-shaped kitchen with deep **Benjamin Moore Essex Green** base cabinets, concealed panels, brass hardware, light countertops, and built-in open shelving inside the Edwardian pie-safe recess. The 30-inch sink sits centered beneath the South window, the counter-depth Haier refrigerator stands on the East wall, and the 30-inch Viking gas range and stainless Zephyr Savona chimney hood anchor the West wall with statement tile to the ceiling (**no upper cabinets at the hood**).

## Room Dimensions & Four Walls
- **Room Footprint (Magicplan MASTER):** **`147" × 126"`**, ceiling height **`121-1/2"`** (East wall `126-1/4"`; confirm finished floor buildup thickness `t ≈ 3/4"` before final markout).
- **West — Oven / Range Run ([Kitchen › Oven / Range Run](#kitchen-oven-range)):** 30-inch **Viking `VGCC5304BSS`** gas range and 30-inch **Zephyr Savona `ZSA-E30AS`** wall-mount chimney hood mounted at **Viking-governed `30"–36"` above the cooking surface (`66"–72"` AFF at `36"` counter)**, dedicated `15A` AFCI `Circuit HD` with `CEC 422.31(B)` breaker lock-off, `6"` rigid vertical metal duct, and ceiling-height statement tile.
- **South & East — Sink, Pie-Safe & Refrigerator Runs ([Kitchen › Sink & North Wall Runs](#kitchen-sink-north)):** South window wall (`0"–115"` from SE finished corner: corner base `0"–36"`, trash pull-out `36"–51"`, 24-inch dishwasher `51"–75"`, window-centered 30-inch sink base, and built-in pie-safe open shelves) + East wall counter-depth **Haier `QJS15HYRFS`** refrigerator return (`88"` run).
- **North — Hall Opening & `D04` Interface ([Kitchen › Sink & North Wall Runs](#kitchen-sink-north)):** Cased opening to hallway (`29-3/8" × 84"`), 3-gang entry switch bank at `48"` AFF, and `D04` dining-side subpanel wall framing.

## Electrical & Plumbing Summary
- **9 Kitchen Circuits (from `D04` Subpanel, Romex with AFCI/GFCI):** Three `20A` countertop circuits, `20A` refrigerator, dishwasher, disposal, gas range ignition, dedicated `15A` AFCI range hood `Circuit HD` (`422.31(B)` lock-off), and kitchen lighting.
- **Plumbing & Gas:** 30-inch sink drain/supplies and dishwasher hookups on the South wall (`#kitchen-sink-north`); `1/2"` gas shutoff valve for the Viking range on the West wall (`#kitchen-oven-range`).
