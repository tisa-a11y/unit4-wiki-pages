# Living & Dining

## Concept
Picture-frame mouldings on every wall. The new electrical panel sits in the dining-room wall.

## Walls, outlets and trim
- **Wall labels:** dining D01–D04, living L01–L06, same as the drawings.
- **Panel wall (D04):** frame in the old doorway with solid backing. Leave room for cable from the side.
- **Outlets:** boxes and empty conduit only. No wire yet.
- **Pocket doors:** stay out of the door cavities.
- **Mouldings:** do not cut any yet.
