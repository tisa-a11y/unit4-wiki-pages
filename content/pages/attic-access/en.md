<section id="sec-attic-notes" class="u4-sec-block">
  <h2>Five Carpentry &amp; Air-Sealing Rules</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Fire-rating &amp; joist check before any cut:</strong> Location is outside Front Door 4 on the common landing (<code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> finished clear). Do not cut or issue a wood-kit rough opening until ceiling fire-rating and joist spacing are verified on site; if fire-rated, order a listed fire-rated door with a full <strong><code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> clear opening</strong>.</li>
    <li><strong>Air-sealed frame &amp; insulated lid:</strong> Caulk the fixed outer frame with <code>3/8&quot;</code> backer rod; install continuous <code>1/2&quot; &times; 1/8&quot;</code> closed-cell foam weatherstrip on clean <code>1&times;2</code> ledger strips; keep the removable lid seam <strong>uncaulked</strong> (test-open twice); secure <code>2</code> unfaced fiberglass batts to the back of the lid with <code>3</code> loose <code>16</code>-gauge wire loops.</li>
    <li><strong>Four-sided dam &amp; clear latches:</strong> Build a <code>4</code>-sided plywood dam <code>2&quot;</code> higher than loose attic insulation, and mount all latches <strong>outside</strong> the <code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> clear opening.</li>
    <li><strong>Continuous <code>24&quot;</code> walkway &amp; <code>30&quot; &times; 30&quot;</code> platform:</strong> Screw down a continuous <code>24&quot;</code> wood walkway (<code>15.8 ft</code>, <code>3</code> sheets of <code>23/32&quot;</code> OSB or <code>3/4&quot;</code> structural plywood &mdash; never <code>3/8&quot;</code>) leading to the level <code>30&quot; &times; 30&quot;</code> wood service platform (<code>1</code> sheet) on the North service side of the furnace.</li>
    <li><strong>Keep temporary bedroom-closet opening separate:</strong> Leave the temporary bedroom-closet ceiling opening open until both the low-voltage thermostat cable and permanent <code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> hatch are finished (<a href="https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6j3Rf89g8M6hPPx7" target="_blank" rel="noopener">task</a>), and confirm the flat-roof leak repair is dry before placing insulation.</li>
  </ol>
</section>
