<section id="sec-attic-notes" class="u4-sec-block">
  <h2>Four Carpentry &amp; Lid Rules (Unconditioned Door 4 Landing)</h2>
  <div class="u4-field-rules">
    <section class="u4-field-rule"><p><strong>Fire-rating &amp; joist check before any cut:</strong> Location is outside Front Door 4 on the unconditioned common stair landing (<code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> finished clear). </p><p>Do not cut or issue a wood-kit rough opening until ceiling fire-rating and joist spacing are verified with Serge &amp; Alex at the rescheduled planning visit; if fire-rated, order a listed fire-rated door with a full <strong><code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> clear opening</strong>.</p></section>
    <section class="u4-field-rule"><p><strong>Dust-sealed frame &amp; uninsulated <code>3/4&quot;</code> lid (no batts or dam):</strong> Because the landing outside Door 4 is unconditioned (<a href="https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/CAEC2025P1/subchapter-10-multifamily-buildings-mandatory-requirements" target="_blank" rel="noopener">2025 CA Energy Code &sect;160.1(a)</a> applies only between conditioned and unconditioned spaces), zero lid insulation, wire loops, screw eyes, or perimeter dam are used. </p><p>Caulk the fixed outer frame with <code>3/8&quot;</code> backer rod, apply continuous <code>1/2&quot; &times; 1/8&quot;</code> closed-cell foam gasket on clean <code>1&times;2</code> ledger strips. </p><p>Keep the removable <code>3/4&quot;</code> plywood lid seam <strong>uncaulked</strong> (test-open twice), and mount all compression latches <strong>outside</strong> the <code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> clear opening.</p></section>
    <section class="u4-field-rule"><p><strong>Continuous <code>24&quot;</code> walkway &amp; <code>30&quot; &times; 30&quot;</code> platform:</strong> Screw down a continuous <code>24&quot;</code> wood walkway (<code>15.8 ft</code>, <code>3</code> sheets of <code>23/32&quot;</code> OSB or <code>3/4&quot;</code> structural plywood &mdash; never <code>3/8&quot;</code>) leading to the level <code>30&quot; &times; 30&quot;</code> wood service platform (<code>1</code> sheet) on the North service side of the furnace. </p><p>NEXT will confirm any equipment-specific mounting requirement before anyone buys or installs pads.</p></section>
    <section class="u4-field-rule"><p><strong>Keep NW bedroom-closet opening separate &amp; agree on attic light wiring:</strong> Leave the temporary NW bedroom-closet ceiling opening open until both the low-voltage thermostat cable and permanent <code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> hatch are finished (<a href="https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6j3Rf89g8M6hPPx7" target="_blank" rel="noopener">task</a>). </p><p>Agree with NEXT&apos;s electrician who wires the permanent switched LED attic light, and confirm the flat-roof leak repair is dry before closing ceilings.</p></section>
  </div>
