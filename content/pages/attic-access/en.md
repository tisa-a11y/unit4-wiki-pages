<h2 id="sec-attic-notes">Five Carpentry & Air-Sealing Rules</h2>

1. **Fire-rating & joist check first:** If the common landing ceiling requires a fire-rated hatch, order a listed fire-rated door with a full **`24" × 36"` clear opening** instead of building the wood kit (avoid nominal `24"×36"` metal doors that shrink clear passage).
2. **Air-sealed frame & insulated lid:** Caulk the fixed outer frame with `3/8"` backer rod; install continuous `1/2" × 1/8"` closed-cell foam weatherstrip on clean `1×2` ledger strips; keep the removable lid seam **uncaulked** (test-open twice); secure `2` unfaced fiberglass batts to the back of the lid with `3` loose `16`-gauge wire loops.
3. **Four-sided dam & clear latches:** Build a `4`-sided plywood dam `2"` higher than loose attic insulation, and mount all latches **outside** the `24" × 36"` clear opening.
4. **Continuous `24"` walkway & `30" × 30"` platform:** Screw down a continuous `24"` wood walkway (`15.8 ft`, `3` sheets of `23/32"` OSB or `3/4"` structural plywood — never `3/8"`) leading to the level `30" × 30"` wood service platform (`1` sheet) on the North service side of the furnace.
5. **Hold bedroom-closet ceiling open:** Keep the temporary bedroom-closet ceiling opening open until both the low-voltage thermostat cable and permanent `24" × 36"` hatch are finished ([task](https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6j3Rf89g8M6hPPx7)), and confirm the flat-roof leak repair is dry before placing insulation.
