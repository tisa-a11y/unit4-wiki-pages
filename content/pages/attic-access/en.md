# Attic Access

## Concept
The first priority is to mark the permanent hatch on the common stair landing outside Front Door 4, west of the hallway. The selected opening is 24" × 36" finished clear. In the attic: a wood walkway, a level platform at the furnace, a light and an outlet. Open the [Permanent Attic Hatch Interactive Plan](./plans/attic-hatch/index.html) or coordinate layout with the [Attic Furnace &amp; Ducts Plan](./plans/hvac-layout/index.html).

## Five rules before any cutting
1. **Mark the opening and document the route.** John marks the finished-clear rectangle and offsets from fixed edges. Photograph the joists, bay, wires and pipes above, plus the lift and turn route. Wait for ceiling assembly, fire protection and framing approval before cutting.
2. **Seal and insulate the hatch.** Follow the attic hatch guide.
3. **Keep the opening clear.** Nothing sticks into the 24" × 36" opening.
4. **Leave the bedroom closet ceiling open** until the switch cable is in and the hatch exists.
5. **Fix leaks first.** Close nothing while the roof leaks.

**Ozone machine:** Attic placement is pending. Keep it off and unplugged during placement.

## Walkway, platform and power
- **Walkway:** 24" wide, 20 ft at most. 23/32" OSB or 3/4" plywood. Never 3/8".
- **Platform:** level, 30" × 30", on the furnace service side.
- **Light:** LED with a switch at the hatch.
- **Outlet:** 120V GFCI within 25 ft. It stays live when the furnace is off.
