## Concept
A clean, air-sealed, insulated **`24" × 36"` finished-clear** permanent attic access with a safe **`24"` continuous wood walkway (`≤ 20 ft`)**, level **`30" × 30"` service platform**, entrance-switched enclosed **LED utility light**, and **line-side 120V GFCI service outlet** ready for NEXT's Carrier/Bryant Legacy furnace.

## Special Notes: Attic Enclosure
**All 5 enclosure rules below govern any attic hatch or temporary ceiling closure. Do NOT buy hatch lumber or cut the ceiling until Gates 1, 4, and 5 are resolved.**

1. **Ceiling fire-rating assembly check BEFORE buying or cutting the custom wood kit:**
   - Confirm first whether the candidate ceiling (Door 4 landing vs. interior hall) forms part of a protected stair/corridor or other fire-rated separation ([R5 p. 2](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf), [R5 p. 5](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf)).
   - If a fire-rated hatch is required, install the accepted complete listed fire-rated ceiling assembly preserving the full **`24" × 36"` finished-clear** passage; the proposed custom wood kit and its `27-1/2" × 39-1/2"` ceiling cut do **not** apply.
   - Also inspect joists from inside the existing attic access and confirm bay width `B` (`B = 27-1/2"` fits; `B > 27-1/2"` uses designed solid side backing; `B < 27-1/2"` requires a resolved structural plan or alternate spot — never shrink `24" × 36"` clear) before any ceiling cut ([R5 p. 5](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf)).

2. **Continuous air-seal & thermal barrier ([R5 p. 3](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf), [R5 p. 6](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf), [R5 p. 8](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf)):**
   - **Fixed-frame gap:** Press **`3/8"` backer rod** into the frame-to-ceiling gap and finish with **paintable sealant** around the fixed frame.
   - **Ledge weatherstrip:** Apply continuous **`1/2" × 1/8"` closed-cell weatherstrip** on clean `1×2` lid ledges (do not stretch around corners; keep weatherstrip clean of paint and caulk).
   - **Uncaulked removable lid seam:** Leave the removable lid perimeter seam **completely uncaulked** and free of bridging paint so the hatch stays operable (test-open twice).
   - **Lid insulation & retention:** Cover the attic face of the lid with **two unfaced fiberglass batts** (`12-5/8" × 37-1/4"` cut from `15" × 48"` batts) matching the attic R-value/thickness, held in place with **3 loose 16-gauge galvanized wire loops** and **6 screw eyes (`≤ 1/2"` threaded shanks** so screws never pierce the painted ceiling face and batts remain lofted, not compressed).
   - **4-sided plywood insulation dam:** Install a **4-sided plywood insulation dam** (from walkway offcuts) around the outside of the frame standing **`2"` above any loose-fill insulation** so loose fill cannot spill into the hatch or living space.

3. **Proposed recessed `~1-1/2"` wood lid finish (pending owner OK) & clear compression latches ([R5 p. 3](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf), [R5 p. 6](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf)):**
   - The R5 wood-kit detail places the removable `3/4"` plywood lid recessed **`~1-1/2"`** above the ceiling face (on `1×2` ledges standing `1-1/2"` vertical + `1/8"` compressed weatherstrip), finished with flat `1×3` square-butt trim and two recessed folding ring pulls (through-bolted with bolt ends covered under the insulation). **This recessed wood-cover finish is proposed only and remains pending explicit owner approval** before cutting or fabrication.
   - Both quarter-turn panel **compression latches/fasteners** (with retained cams/keepers; select grip range only after measuring actual lid + ledge thickness) and all fixed hardware, ledges, and trim must sit **completely outside the `24" × 36"` finished-clear passage**. Demonstrate controlled lift, tilt, and safe lid parking on the flat landing away from the stair edge.

4. **Temporary bedroom closet enclosure hold ([R5 p. 5](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf), [R5 p. 8](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf)):**
   - **Step 1 first:** John measures and photographs the existing open temporary bedroom-closet ceiling (clear width × length, joist/framing direction & spacing, headroom, and obstructions) under [](https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6j3j3pXFrFWhH2w7) before any closet framing or closure.
   - **NEVER close the temporary bedroom closet ceiling** until **BOTH** (a) the low-voltage wall-switch cable is run up to the attic **AND** (b) the permanent `24" × 36"` attic hatch exists, with actual furnace passage coordinated with the crew and NEXT ([](https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6j3Rf89g8M6hPPx7)). Retaining a smaller auxiliary closet hatch remains a separate open owner decision.

5. **Active roof/attic leak repair gate before enclosing or insulating ([R5 p. 8](https://tisa-a11y.github.io/unit4-wiki-pages/wiki-docs/attic-hatch-r5/Attic-hatch-practical-guide_R5_EN.pdf)):**
   - Tisa reported active main-roof / attic leaking ([`2026-11-19_0358_field_roof-attic_active-leak.md`](../to-triage/2026-11-19_0358_field_roof-attic_active-leak.md) — urgent and separate from the deferred 4 rooftop hood exhaust ducts [](https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6j2X5f4W65jvCmpf)).
   - Diagnose and repair any active roof/attic leak affecting the hatch, walkway, platform, or closet area **before** enclosing ceilings or installing attic/hatch insulation.
