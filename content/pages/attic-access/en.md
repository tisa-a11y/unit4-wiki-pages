# Attic Access

## Concept
Permanent hatch in the hallway ceiling, 24" × 36" clear. In the attic: a wood walkway, a level platform at the furnace, a light and an outlet.

## Five rules before any cutting
1. **Check the ceiling first.** No cutting until the fire-rating check and the joist measurement are done.
2. **Seal and insulate the hatch.** Follow the attic hatch guide.
3. **Keep the opening clear.** Nothing sticks into the 24" × 36" opening.
4. **Leave the bedroom closet ceiling open** until the switch cable is in and the hatch exists.
5. **Fix leaks first.** Close nothing while the roof leaks.

## Walkway, platform and power
- **Walkway:** 24" wide, 20 ft at most. 23/32" OSB or 3/4" plywood. Never 3/8".
- **Platform:** level, 30" × 30", on the furnace service side.
- **Light:** LED with a switch at the hatch.
- **Outlet:** 120V GFCI within 25 ft. It stays live when the furnace is off.
