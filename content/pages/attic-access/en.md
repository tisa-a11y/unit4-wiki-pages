# HVAC › Attic Access

## Concept
Air-sealed, insulated **`24"×36"` finished-clear** permanent hallway hatch with a `24"` wood walkway (`≤ 20 ft`), level `30"×30"` platform, switched LED light, and live 120V GFCI.

## 5 Enclosure Rules (Verify Before Cutting)
1. **Fire-Rating & Bay `B` Check First:** Confirm whether ceiling requires a listed fire-rated assembly AND verify joist bay width `B` (`27-1/2"` proposed wood cut) before buying lumber or cutting.
2. **Continuous Air-Seal & Thermal Barrier:** `3/8"` backer rod + sealant on fixed frame; `1/2"×1/8"` closed-cell weatherstrip on `1×2` ledges; leave removable lid seam **uncaulked**; retain 2 R-matched unfaced fiberglass batts with 3 loose 16-ga wire loops (`≤ 1/2"` screw eyes); build 4-sided plywood dam `2"` above loose fill.
3. **Clear Latches:** Keep all latches/hardware completely outside the `24"×36"` clear chute (recessed lid finish pending owner OK).
4. **Bedroom Closet Hold:** Never close the open bedroom closet ceiling until low-voltage switch cable **AND** permanent `24"×36"` hatch exist.
5. **Zero Active Leaks:** Fix any active roof/attic leaks before enclosing or insulating.

## Walkway, Platform & Electrical Specs
- **Walkway (`CMC 304.4`):** `66"` attic height caps run at **`≤ 20 ft`**, `24"` wide (`30"` clear passage). Use **`23/32"` rated OSB** (cheapest compliant) or `3/4"` ply / `2×` (**never `3/8"`**; 1 strip per 4×8 sheet; `#9 × 2-1/2"` or `3"` wood screws into framing).
- **Platform (`CMC 304.4.3`):** Level **`30"×30"`** min screwed to framing on service side (**no vibration pads from John**). Type B vent `≥ 1"` clearance.
- **Electrical:** Enclosed LED light + entrance switch + **120V GFCI within `25 ft`** wired **live on line side** independent of furnace disconnect.
