Special Notes: Attic Enclosure

  • 1. Ceiling fire-rating check first: Confirm whether the ceiling/landing requires a fire-rated assembly BEFORE buying wood or cutting (custom shop-built wood hatch vs fire-rated ceiling access assembly).
  • 2. Continuous air-seal + thermal barrier: Seal fixed frame to ceiling with 3/8" backer rod + sealant; install 1/2" × 1/8" closed-cell weatherstrip on ledges; leave the removable lid seam uncaulked; retain 2 unfaced fiberglass batts (matching attic R-value) with 3 loose 16-ga wire loops + ≤ 1/2" screw eyes; build a 4-sided plywood loose-fill dam 2" above attic insulation.
  • 3. Clear latches: Keep all latches and hardware completely outside the 24" × 36" clear chute.
  • 4. Temporary bedroom closet access: Keep the open bedroom closet ceiling open until low-voltage switch cable is installed AND the permanent hatch exists.
  • 5. Active leaks pre-gate: Fix any active roof or attic leaks before enclosing framing or insulation.
