# Bathroom

## Concept
A bright, classic bathroom: marble-look floor tile, glossy white subway tile on the walls, warm bronze fixtures, a drop-in tub, and a medicine cabinet mounted on the wall surface.

## Room and fixtures
- **Room size:** 72-1/8" × 82-1/8". Pedestal sink on the left, toilet on the right, tub across the back wall with the window on the left and the controls on the right.
- **Tub:** Kohler 66" × 32" drop-in, installed and leak-tested Nov 9.
- **Fixtures:** all metal is Champagne Bronze. The toilet has a 10" rough-in. The fan duct is done.
- **Medicine cabinet:** mounted on the wall surface, not recessed.
- **Door:** the pocket door is on hold. Do not frame it or buy the track. A hinged door that swings out is being looked at; the 28" door is kept.

## Tile and order of work
- **Window trim first.** Put the original window trim on over the HardieBoard before any wall tile goes up.
- **Floor:** the tile is laid on the Schluter membrane and is not grouted yet. Check the toilet flange, then grout with Warm Gray. The base is matching 3" × 12" bullnose.
- **Walls:** HardieBoard and both niches are up (main niche 12-1/2" × 20", soap niche 10" × 10"). The wall tile is white 3" × 6" subway.
- **Electrical:** two 20-amp GFCI circuits for the outlets (BATH-R) and one 15-amp circuit for lights and fan (BATH-L). The ceiling wire runs straight to a junction box in the attic, with no empty conduit.
- **Models:** Kohler K-1821 tub · Delta Arcadia 144892-CZ-HS valve · Panasonic FV-0511VQC1 fan · STAINMASTER Calacatta Gem 12×24 matte floor tile (SM421224XTMTLW) · Daltile Restore Bright White 3×6 wall tile (RE1536MODHD1P4).
