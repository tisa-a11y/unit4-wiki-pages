# Paint & Floors

## Concept
One set of colors and floors for the whole unit: marble-look tile in the bathroom, restored hardwood in the rooms, matte tile in the kitchen, and matching trim and wall paint.

## Floors and paint
- **Bathroom floor:** Calacatta Gem 12" × 24" matte tile is laid. Check the toilet flange, then grout with Warm Gray.
- **Kitchen floor:** confirm the thickness of the matte tile floor (about 3/4") before the final cabinet marks.
- **Bedroom floor:** patch the missing piece of hardwood before trim.
- **Paint colors are not chosen yet.** Under review: Benjamin Moore Essex Green (HC-188) for the kitchen lower cabinets, Sherwin-Williams Urbane Bronze (SW 7048), Benjamin Moore Pale Oak (OC-20) and Benjamin Moore Chantilly Lace (OC-65). Prime and paint after the drywall is closed.
