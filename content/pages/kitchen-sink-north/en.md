# South Sink & North Wall Runs

## Concept
South wall with the sink and a former pie-safe recess to weatherproof and cover. East wall with the refrigerator. North wall with the hall opening and the panel wall.

## Walls and measurements
- **South wall, from the southeast corner:** 36" corner, 12" trash pull-out, 24" dishwasher, then 30" sink base. Using the same southeast zero, the plan-derived dishwasher station is 48"–72" and the sink is 72"–102", center 87". The former 51"–75" dishwasher and 75"–105" / center 90" sink coordinates are retired, not installation directions. Confirm the common zero, window center and outside edge of rear-door casing on site.
- **Wait:** do not set the sink cabinet, move the drain or fix electrical boxes yet. The 12" base does not select a 12" end filler; keep the 6" end option and verify the physical passage before deciding.
- **Old pie-safe:** inspect the backing and weather barrier, fix damage and water entry, and photograph the sealing before covering the recess.
- **East wall:** 36" corner, 18" drawer base and 28" refrigerator bay give a calculated 82" run. The upper beside the refrigerator is 30" wide. Keep 2" clear behind the refrigerator and the space above it open.
- **Counter outlets:** 41" from the floor.
- **Full kitchen plan:** Check L-layout stations and narrowest passage across all 4 walls in the [Interactive Full Kitchen Floor Plan](./plans/kitchen-floor-plan/index.html) before cutting or ordering.
- **North wall:** three light switches at 48". Do not close the panel wall.
