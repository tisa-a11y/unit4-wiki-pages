# Sink & North Wall Runs

## Concept
South wall with the sink and pie-safe shelves. East wall with the refrigerator. North wall with the hall opening and the panel wall.

## Walls and measurements
- **South wall, from the southeast corner:** corner cabinet 0"–36". Trash pull-out 36"–51". Dishwasher 51"–75". Then the 30" sink cabinet under the window.
- **Sink position is not settled.** Site marks say 72"–102". The earlier plan says 75"–105". Do not set the sink cabinet or move the drain.
- **By the door:** compare a 6" filler and a 12" filler. Check the walking space.
- **Pie-safe shelves:** about 26"–59" from the corner, top about 80" from the floor. Outlets beside it, never under it.
- **East wall (88"):** 36" corner cabinet, 24" cabinet, 28" refrigerator space. Leave 2" behind the refrigerator. No cabinet above it.
- **Counter outlets:** 41" from the floor.
- **North wall:** three light switches at 48". Do not close the panel wall.
