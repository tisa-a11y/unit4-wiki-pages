# Sink & North Wall Runs

## Concept
The South wall with the sink under the window and the pie-safe shelves, the East wall with the refrigerator, and the North wall with the hall opening, the light switches and the framing for the electrical panel.

## Walls and measurements
- **South wall, measured from the southeast corner:** corner cabinet 0"–36", trash pull-out 36"–51", 24" dishwasher 51"–75", then the 30" sink cabinet under the window, then a filler or pull-out up to the door casing.
- **The sink position is not settled.** The marks on site put the sink cabinet at 72"–102". The earlier plan says 75"–105", centered at 90". Do not set the sink cabinet or move the drain until the layout photos are reviewed.
- **Walkway by the door:** compare a 6" filler with a 12" one and check the walking space, counting the countertop overhang.
- **Pie-safe shelves:** the old recess stays, about 26"–59" from the southeast corner, with its top about 80" from the floor. Counter outlets go beside it, at 18" and 66" from the corner and 41" from the floor. Never under it.
- **East wall (88"):** a 36" corner cabinet, a 24" cabinet, then a 28" space for the refrigerator (27-9/16" wide; leave 2" behind it; no cabinet above it). Counter outlets at 12" and 48" from the southeast corner, 41" from the floor.
- **North wall:** the hall opening (29-3/8" × 84"), three light switches at 48" from the floor, and the framing for the panel wall (D04). Do not close that wall yet.
- **Plumbing:** the sink supply, the drain and the dishwasher connections go inside the 30" sink cabinet.
- **Model:** Haier QJS15HYRFS refrigerator.
