# Hallway

## Concept
The hallway has the attic hatch in the ceiling, wall mouldings, and the trim around the hidden laundry door.

## What belongs here
- **Attic hatch:** do not cut the ceiling yet. See [Attic Access](#attic-access).
- **Laundry door:** the door is on [Laundry Closet](#laundry). Here, only the trim around it.
- **Before patching and paint:** rough-in the outlets, the lights and the hatch light switch.
