## Questions for Serge and Alex at the Planning Visit

- **Hatch:** Confirm the 24"×36" clear markout outside Door 4, joists and ceiling fire protection. Photograph the markout; do not cut yet. Check the furnace’s 14.19"×29" end-first carry route and duct access ([hatch task](https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6j3RvWmJ329qPCc7)).
- **Panel:** Confirm NEXT can use John’s mounted Eaton 125A D04 box and identify feeder entry. Obtain electrician sign-off for the 80A feed and conditional 100A main breaker before wiring or closing the wall ([panel task](https://app.todoist.com/app/task/6hvRcQ4XC9mr56g7)).
- **Exhaust:** Confirm horizontal sidewall clearance: ≥4 ft to the lot line, comparing South wall with West rear-yard wall. No flat-roof cuts.
- **Light and mounting:** Agree who connects the permanent switched LED attic light and when. Confirm furnace-specific mounting/vibration hardware before buying pads, and list attic/wall preparations needed before NEXT starts.

  <ul>
    <li><strong>Temporary NW bedroom-closet opening reference:</strong> <code>X=315.4&quot;&ndash;352.0&quot;, Y=5.4&quot;&ndash;88.0&quot;</code>.</li>
    <li><strong>Furnace fit (<code>33.25&quot; &times; 14.19&quot; &times; 29&quot;</code>):</strong> Passes end-first (<code>14.19&quot; &times; 29&quot;</code>) through the full <code>24&quot; &times; 36&quot;</code> clear opening.
