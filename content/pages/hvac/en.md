# HVAC

## What NEXT HVAC installs (furnace &amp; heating only)
- **Interactive plan:** Open the [Attic Furnace &amp; Ducts Interactive Layout](./plans/hvac-layout/index.html) or see [Attic Access](#attic-access).
- **Furnace:** gas furnace in the attic. Cabinet 33.25" × 14.19" × 29". Fits through the 24" × 36" hatch.
- **Ducts:** three supply, one return, one jump duct. Vent through the roof. Kitchen range hood exhaust is NOT in NEXT HVAC's scope (stays with our crew).
- **Permits and tests:** NEXT HVAC.
- **Our crew:** 24" walkway (20 ft max), level 30" × 30" platform, LED light + live GFCI, drywall, stucco and paint repair. No vibration pads needed (NEXT supplies).
